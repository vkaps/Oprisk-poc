How to read it

  • Loss is what the firm publicly lost or paid for the event: trading loss, fines, settlements, compensation or stolen funds, as reported. The breakdown is under each event. Several fines for one case at one firm are one event.
  • Back-office control failure = Y when the loss came from a failed operational control: processing, reconciliation, confirmation, settlement, custody and client money, reporting, surveillance, data, IT, limits or valuation checks. Benchmark rigging, rogue concealment and client-pricing conduct are N. Internal fraud is a separate column, so you can keep or drop those events for your separate exercise.
  • USD uses approximate average exchange rates of the year announced (within about 5%). No inflation adjustment.
  • Near misses (amber) had a large exposure but little or no final loss, such as a payment recalled the next day. They are kept because they are scenario material.
  • Years undetected is the time from the start of the misconduct or error to its discovery, where public.
  • Excluded: pure market-risk losses with no operational failure (LTCM, Amaranth), anti-money-laundering and sanctions fines, and retail mis-selling.
  • Official record is the regulator's, prosecutor's, court's or the firm's own publication. Events numbered OD come from a sweep of regulators' enforcement registers (FCA, SEC, CFTC, Federal Reserve, OCC, FINRA, Korea FSC) for control failures of $10m+. Events numbered SM are the $1m–10m tier: every FCA fine of $1m+ since 2013 on a markets or back-office topic, plus a targeted (not complete) sweep of SEC, FINRA, CFTC, ASIC, SFC, BaFin, AMF and the Central Bank of Ireland. Older cases known only from press reports say "none located online".
  • Internal fraud is flagged (Basel event type) and can be hidden, leaving control failures, errors, external events and conduct.
  • Confidence: High means the amount was checked against an official or primary source; Medium relies on a secondary compilation; Low is an estimate.
  • The full table is in the project repository as scenarios/extreme_events.csv.
